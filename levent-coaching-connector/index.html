Levent Coaching Connector

Levent Coaching Connector is a personal, owner-operated software tool run by its owner. It is used by one person, the owner, with the owner's own Google account, for a private training workflow. It is not a public product, has no customers, has no sign-up, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

What it does

The tool uses a private Google Drive shared-drive mailbox controlled for the owner's workflow. The owner's Google account, through the consent screen, is used only to read the permission flags of one folder that the application's provisioning process created in that mailbox (for example, whether the account may rename it, and may not share or delete it). The private mailbox system also uses two dedicated service accounts, described in the Privacy Policy.

What it asks Google for

The consent screen asks the owner for only one permission, shown by Google as "See information about your Google Drive files" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly). That owner-authorized credential is used solely to read permission flags for one receipt folder by exact ID. It does not request file or folder names, does not read the contents of any file, and does not list, search, change, create, share or delete anything. The two dedicated service accounts are not authorized through the consent screen; see the Privacy Policy, section 3.

Who uses it

Only the owner, with the owner's own Google account. If you reached a Google consent screen for this application and you are not the owner, do not approve it.
